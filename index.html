A DIGITAL BIOGRAPHY

Bakhsh Ali Lakho

Industrial Leadership | Education | Sports Administration | Social Service

Bakhsh Ali Lakho is documented in published biographical sources for his professional work in the oil and ghee industry, educational initiatives, volunteerism, and sports administration in Sindh, Pakistan.

Born in 1939 in Lakho Wada village, Khairpur Mirs, he is described by Encyclopaedia Sindhiana as a social reformer. This page brings together the details reported about his education, career and public service.

04SGA

Sindh Graduates Association

The Sindh Graduates Association (SGA) is a volunteer organization with an educational and community-development focus.
